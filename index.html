Muscle age is a number you can move

A birthday counts down. Muscle age reports status. The HackedSelf strength piece names three field tests for the gap: grip strength on a dynamometer, gait speed, and lower-body power. Each returns a number with published bands, and the bands read older or younger than your years. That gap is the actual target of the training. The three older articles on the site, the sauna protocol, the semaglutide evidence audit, and the XPRIZE Healthspan rules audit, all circle the same fact: the costs of aging compound in the second half of the fifth decade, and the checks you skip at 40 are the ones that find it.

The rig costs less than a supplement bottle

A cheap hand dynamometer runs about $22. Spread over a 40-year training horizon that is 55 cents a year, which makes it the cheapest instrument in the whole biohacking stack. The protocol matters more than the purchase: same hand, same elbow angle, seated with the elbow at 90 degrees, two or three tries per hand, keep the best. Ten minutes, once a month, at a desk. A number only tracks age if the measurement itself never drifts, so the fixed routine is the entire value.

Test the pair, read the pattern

Grip strength drops when the muscle side weakens and it also drops when the nerve side does. Gait speed separates the two. A grip dip with a clean gait is a training signal; grip and gait sliding together is a doctor question. Add a lower-body power check, chair-rise time is the no-equipment version, and the battery covers the three declines that matter. It is the same habit the site applies to protocols: fixed dose, fixed cadence, no freelancing on the method.

At 40 the tests feel boring, because almost anyone who lifts reads fit for age. Their job is the baseline. When the 50s curve starts to bend, the comparison that means something is against a number recorded on a clean starting line, from a dynamometer that always squeezed the same way. Muscle age is one of the few numbers in this space that training actually moves. Record it before you need it.
